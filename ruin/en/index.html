Work order WO-0147

The coolant pump on berth B-01 has stopped. The part has arrived at locker A-17. Nobody has gone for it.

Mia, the berth dispatcher, gives you the job. You collect a lockout key in Stores and write your name on the tag. You shut the V-12 feed valve in Engine Access. You calibrate the part on the test stand in Lab 2. Then the pump: isolate, replace, connect the coolant path, run the test. 1.8 A, 1800 rpm, 8 L/min. The run record is recertified. Mia signs. Pay is 80 cr.

What is in it

  • About 10 minutes, start to finish. It ends.
  • One repair, done in order: lockout key, feed valve, calibration, isolation, replacement, test run, recertification, report.
  • Six rooms to walk: Observation, Berth Deck, Crew Quarters, Lab 2, Stores, Engine Access.
  • First person or third person. Switch at any time.
  • Twelve small things to pick up. One line each. None of them is a lore entry.
  • One dispatcher. Mia is brief, a little tired, and fair. She pays when the record is signed.

These lines describe the build in development. Any line not confirmed in the release build will be removed.

What it is not

No combat. No clock on screen. No way to fail; a wrong try costs nothing. Nobody explains the world.
